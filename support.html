Questions & answers

Sort & Earn is a digital waste management platform that helps residential societies segregate recyclable waste, schedule collections and generate earnings for the society fund.

Residents segregate recyclable waste at source. The society schedules a pickup through Sort & Earn. Connected collectors collect the material, sell it through recycling channels and the resulting earnings are added to the society fund.

Common recyclable materials include paper, cardboard, plastic bottles, metal cans and glass. Wet or biodegradable waste should generally be kept separate according to your society's waste collection rules.

No. In the society-based model, earnings from recyclable waste are credited to the society fund rather than being paid directly to individual residents.

Segregated recyclable waste is collected and sold through recycling channels. The value generated from these materials contributes to the society fund.

Society management can schedule a collection by using the pickup option on the platform. The request can then be assigned to a connected local collector.

Mixed or contaminated recyclable waste may have a lower recovery value. Proper segregation helps collectors and recyclers recover more useful material and improves the overall value generated for the society.

Yes. The platform is designed to connect participating societies with local collectors so that segregated recyclable material can be collected efficiently.

Yes. The pickup status section can show information such as the pickup number, scheduled time and current status of the collection.

Segregation prevents recyclable materials from becoming contaminated by wet waste. It makes recycling easier, reduces landfill waste and can create additional value from recyclable materials.

Terms & Conditions

1. Platform Purpose

Sort & Earn is designed to facilitate responsible waste segregation, recyclable waste collection and society-level incentive management.

2. Society Participation

Societies using the platform are responsible for providing accurate information and following applicable waste management rules.

3. Waste Segregation

Residents should segregate waste according to the categories supported by their society and collection partners.

4. Society Fund

Earnings generated through recyclable materials are intended for the participating society fund and are not automatically distributed to individual residents.

5. Collector Services

Pickup schedules depend on collector availability and operational conditions.

6. Material Value

The value of recyclable material may vary depending on material type, quantity, quality and prevailing market conditions.

7. Platform Information

Statistics and pickup information displayed in a demo or prototype environment may be illustrative.

8. Responsible Usage

Users should not misuse the platform or provide misleading information.

9. Updates

These terms may be updated as the Sort & Earn platform develops.
